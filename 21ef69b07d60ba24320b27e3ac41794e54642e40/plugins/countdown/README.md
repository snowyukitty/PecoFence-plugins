# Countdown 倒数日

Shows how many days are left until the dates you care about, nearest first. Dates that have passed
show how long ago they were (turn this off in the settings).

Write the dates in the fence menu, Plugin settings, separated by `;`:

```
2026-12-25 Christmas; 01-01 New Year
```

A date with a year (`2026-12-25`) is one day; a date without one (`12-25`) comes back every year.
The fence updates itself at midnight.

Sends nothing over the network and asks for no permissions.

---

显示离你在意的日子还有几天，最近的在最上面。已经过去的日子显示过去了几天（可以在设置里关掉）。
在栅栏菜单的「插件设置」里写日期，用 `;` 隔开，例如 `2026-12-25 圣诞节; 01-01 元旦`。带年份的日期只算那一天，
不带年份的每年都算。不联网，不需要任何权限。
